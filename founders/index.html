The People Behind Sattva Jnana

Meet the Founders of Sri Hari Sattva Jnana

Three mentors, one educational vision — personalised home tuition in Bengaluru, built around every student.

Sri Hari Sattva Jnana — known to most families simply as Sattva Jnana — was founded in Bengaluru by three educators who share one belief: every student learns best with focused, one-on-one attention. Harshith G S, Sahana Shree and Jeevan G bring together Commerce, Mathematics and Science expertise — with Harshith adding AI-certified, AI-driven teaching methods — to lead Sattva Jnana's home tuition programmes, together mentoring 200+ students across grades with personalised, subject-specific guidance delivered at home.

Founder — 01

Harshith G S

Founder & AI-Powered Education Strategist, Sattva Jnana

With a strong academic foundation and a passion for teaching, Harshith G S founded Sri Hari Sattva Jnana with a simple belief — every student deserves individual attention, the right guidance, and a learning experience built around their needs. Alongside 6+ years of teaching experience, Harshith is AI-certified and has built AI-automation tools for the finance industry. He now brings that same AI expertise into education — using AI-driven methods to personalise lesson plans, track each student's progress, and make one-on-one learning sharper and more effective.
