Common Questions

Programme FAQs

How do I know which programme is right for my child?+

Share your child's grade, subjects and current challenges with us, and we'll recommend a programme and matching tutor. A free demo session helps confirm the fit before you commit.

Can we switch programmes later?+

Yes. As the student's needs change — a new grade, a new exam, or a specific subject gap — we can adjust the programme or bring in a different tutor.

How often are sessions held?+

Frequency depends on the programme and the student's goals, from a couple of sessions a week to an intensive daily schedule during board exam preparation. This is planned together with the parent.

Is progress shared with parents?+

Yes. Parents receive regular updates on attendance, topics covered and areas that need more attention, so progress is always visible.

Not Sure Which Programme Is Right?

Tell us a little about the student and we'll recommend the right programme and tutor match.
