Polynomial Expressions

A polynomial is an algebraic expression consisting of variables and coefficients, using addition, subtraction and non-negative integer powers.

Example
3x² + 5x − 6 = 0
Here, 3, 5 and −6 are coefficients and x has power 2.

If x² + 5x + 6 = 0, what are the values of x?
A   2, 3
B   −2, −3
C   −2, 3
D   2, −3
Submit Answer
Good attempt! 🌱 You're on the right track. Let's look at where terms were formed.

Step 1
x² + 5x + 6 = 0

Step 2
Factorise (x + 2)(x + 3) = 0
So, x = −2 or x = −3
